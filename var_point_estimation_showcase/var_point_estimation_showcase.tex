    \section{VAR Point-Estimation Showcase
Notebook}\label{var-point-estimation-showcase-notebook}

This notebook is a \textbf{presentation-style walkthrough} of the VAR
point-estimation project.

It focuses on: - data used in the multivariate setup, - the VAR(2)
design structure, - model families compared under point estimation, -
rolling one-step forecast outputs and risk metrics, - saved artifacts
(CSV + figures).

\subsection{Core design at a glance}\label{core-design-at-a-glance}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Response dimension: \textbf{4} (SPY, QQQ, DIA, IWN returns)
\item
  Lag order: \textbf{p = 2}
\item
  Predictor dimension: \textbf{9}\\

diagnostics (LPS, predictive std, VaR/ES behavior).
